Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Reel Reposter is a personal tool that uploads videos to its owner's own YouTube channel.

What it uses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app asks only for permission to upload videos to YouTube (the youtube.upload scope). The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, store or share personal data about anyone.
  • It does not read, change or delete existing videos, comments or account details.
  • It does not sell data or use it for advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
